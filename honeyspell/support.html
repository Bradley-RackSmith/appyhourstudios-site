How to play

  • Spell: drag across touching letters and let go. Every letter in the word fills with honey. Letters can be used again in other words.
  • Fill the hive: the number at the top left is how many words you have left. Fill every cell before it reaches zero.
  • Stars: finish with words to spare for more stars. The star at the top shows how many to keep spare for three.
  • Long words: a word of six letters or more gives you that word back free.
  • Hint: shows a word that fills empty cells, with its letters numbered in order.
  • Bea's Help: tap it, then any empty cell, and Bea fills that cell.
  • Out of words: watch a short video or spend coins for three more, or try the hive again.

Bea walks you through all this on the first level. You can watch her walkthrough again in the game under Settings > How to play.

Special cells

  • Royal jelly (pink): fill it to earn coins.
  • Wild cell (?): can be any letter. Trace through it and Bea finds the word.
  • Flower: fill it and it pours honey into every cell around it.
  • Wax lid (a 2 in the corner): needs honey from two different words.
  • Lock: opens only for a word of five letters or more.
  • Fog: hides its letter until a cell next to it fills.
  • Busy bee: fill it and she gives you a word back.

Purchases

  • Restoring Remove Ads or the Starter Hive on a new phone or after reinstalling: in the game, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Coins are used up as you spend them, so they can't be restored.
  • The Honey Pot starts with 500 bonus coins and fills up as you finish levels, to 2,000. You can open it from the Shop whenever it holds 500 or more, and every coin in it goes into your balance.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

  • To change your ad consent, open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • To stop ads from using your advertising identifier, go to your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • More in the privacy policy.

Your progress

  • Your levels, stars, coins, Word Jar, garden and outfits are saved on your phone (and in your iPhone's own backup). They don't sync between devices, and deleting the game resets them.
  • A word you're sure is real but the game doesn't accept? Email it to us and we'll add it in an update.
