A word puzzle that fills up

Every hive is a honeycomb of letters. Your job is to fill every cell with honey, using as few words as you can.

Trace

Drag to spell

Slide your finger across touching letters and let go. Every letter in the word fills with honey, and you can use a letter again in another word.

Words left

Make every word count

Each hive gives you a set number of words. Pick words that reach the empty cells, and spell six letters or more to get that word back free.

Stars

Questions

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play, with an ad between some levels (from level 9, never in the middle of a hive) and optional videos for rewards. Optional in-app purchases are a one-time Starter Hive (no ads between levels, 1,500 coins and the Lavender hive), Remove Ads, coin packs and opening your Honey Pot.

Do the levels run out?

No. There are twelve gardens of 20 levels, and more than 1,000 levels in all, with new ideas arriving in updates.

Which words count?

Everyday English words of three letters or more, in US or British spelling. If a word you think is real isn't accepted, tell us at the support address and we'll add it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every puzzle, including the Daily Hive, plays without a connection. Ads need one.

What does the game know about me?

See the privacy policy.

I bought Remove Ads or the Starter Hive. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open the Shop in the game and tap Restore purchases.
