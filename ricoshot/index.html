Shoot through goo.
Pop every slime.

Fling a volley of orbs, bank them off the walls and squelch them straight through glowing Goo Slimes. Every orb comes out coated, and carries that goo's power into whatever it hits next.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free on iPhone. Plays offline. No timers while you aim.

One thumb, dozens of orbs

Drag to aim, let go, and watch the volley bounce through a field of jelly. Pop the slimes before they reach Pip.

Aim

Bank your shots

Drag to aim and let go. Your orbs fire one after another and ricochet off the walls and slimes. The first orb to land sets where Pip shoots from next.

Goo Coating

Shoot through goo

Glowing Goo Slimes don't block your shot. Orbs pass right through and come out coated, and the aim line takes the goo's colour so you can plan the volley.

Powers

A new build every run

Popping slimes fills your star bar. Each level up offers three powers: fire splash, chain lightning, splitting orbs, drill orbs, crits, blasts and more.

Kings

A boss every 10 waves

Every 10 waves a King Slime arrives, twice the size, costing two hearts if it reaches Pip. Then the world changes around you.

Hearts

Three chances

A slime reaching Pip costs a heart, not the run. Lose all three and it's game over, with one chance to keep going.

Worlds

Five places to travel

Gumdrop Meadow, Glowcap Caves, Lava Pudding, Frosty Peaks and Candy Cosmos. How far can you go?

Workshop

Get further every time

Spend the coins you win on upgrades that last: more starting orbs, extra hearts, crits, coins and free powers.

Looks

Dress up Pip

Sunny, Minty, Berry, Ember, Frost and Cosmic. Your orbs match Pip's look.

Four kinds of goo

A coated orb carries its goo for its next three hits. Pop a Goo Slime and its goo splashes over every orb nearby.

From wave 2

Split

Each coated hit sends out a second orb.

From wave 4

Boom

Each coated hit blasts the slimes all around the target.

From wave 6

Toxic

Each coated hit poisons the slime, and it keeps losing health turn after turn.

From wave 9

Punch

Each coated hit does double damage.

Super Upgrades

Buy Supers in the Workshop and bring one into every run. At wave 15 a banner says it's ready, and it switches on. Level one up and it arrives sooner, at wave 12 and then wave 10.

Pom the Pal

A friend fires too

Pip's pal stands across the board and fires a volley of his own, mirroring your aim.

Goo Fountain

Coated from the start

Every third orb leaves Pip already coated in goo.

Bubble Shield

Saved by a bubble

An extra heart, and a bubble that stops the next three slimes to reach Pip.

Mega Orb

Go big

Every shot, your first orb is a giant that drills through slimes for triple damage.

Rebuild Pip's World

The slimes squashed Pip's five worlds flat. Spend the coins you win to build them back up, piece by piece: a cottage and a rainbow, glowing mushrooms, a toffee bridge, an igloo, a candy rocket. Finish a world and a friend moves in. They keep Pip company on the home screen and can join your runs as the Pal, and the world becomes your home screen's background.

Slimes with personality

The number on a slime is its health. Some have tricks.

Helmet Slimes

Hard hats

Their helmet soaks up the first hits before the slime takes any damage.

Splitters

Two for one

Pop one and it bursts into little Slimelets.

Boom Slimes

Handle with care

Pop one and it blows up its neighbours. Good news for you.

Healers

Pop these first

They mend the slimes around them every turn.

Made for everyone

Three difficulties

Relaxed, Normal or Spicy

Relaxed has gentler slimes and two extra hearts, so younger players get far. Spicy is tougher and pays more coins. Each keeps its own best wave.

At your pace

No timers while you aim

Line up every shot as long as you like. Tips appear the first time you meet something new.

Clear to see

Numbers, not just colours

Every slime shows its health, so you never need to tell colours apart.

Anywhere

Plays offline

No account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your phone.

Questions

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play, with an ad between some runs (never in the middle of one) and optional videos for rewards. Optional in-app purchases include a one-time Starter Pack (gems, coins, the Ember look and Pom the Pal), Remove Ads and packs of gems.

Is there a last wave?

No. Slimes keep getting tougher, so every run ends somewhere. The five worlds come round again after wave 50. How far can you go?

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays without a connection.

What does the game know about me?

See the privacy policy.

I bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open the Shop in the game and tap Restore purchases.
