How to play

  • Aim and shoot: drag to aim and let go to fire your orbs. You can also drag below Pip to pull back like a slingshot.
  • Pop slimes: the number on a slime is its health. Hit it down to zero to pop it.
  • More orbs: hit the glowing +1 rings to add an orb to your volley.
  • Goo Slimes: shoot through a glowing Goo Slime to coat your orbs for their next three hits. Split goo sends out extra orbs, Boom goo blasts the slimes around, Toxic goo poisons, and Punch goo does double damage.
  • Powers: popping slimes fills your star bar. Each level up lets you pick one of three powers for the run, with one free re-roll.
  • Hearts: each slime that reaches Pip costs a heart (a King Slime costs two). Lose them all and the run ends, with one chance to continue.
  • Difficulty: pick Relaxed, Normal or Spicy above the Play button. Relaxed is gentler, with two extra hearts. Each keeps its own best wave.
  • Supers: buy them in the Workshop under Supers and equip one. It switches on partway through every run, at wave 15 (sooner at higher levels).
  • Pip's World: tap World on the home screen and spend coins to rebuild Pip's five worlds. Each item opens at a best wave, on any difficulty. Finish a world and a friend moves in.
  • Long volleys: tap Speed up to hurry them along, or Call back to bring every orb home.

The rules are also in the game under Settings > How to play.

Purchases

  • Restoring Remove Ads or the Starter Pack on a new phone or after reinstalling: in the game, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Gems are used up as you spend them, so they can't be restored.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

  • Your coins, gems, upgrades, Supers, Pip's World, looks and best waves are saved on your phone (and in your iPhone's own backup). They don't sync between devices, and deleting the game resets them.
  • Stuck on a wave? Spend your coins in the Workshop: more starting orbs and extra hearts make the next run go further.
