Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Ricoshot: Slime Siege is a game for iPhone made by Appy Hour Studios, LLC. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any personal data. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, gems, Workshop upgrades, Pip's looks, best wave, stats, daily gift streak and settings are stored on your phone. We never see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads

The game shows an ad between some runs and offers optional videos for rewards. These ads are shown by the game itself. No information about you is sent to an advertising company, and the game doesn't ask to track you.

Purchases

The Starter Pack, Remove Ads and gem packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment details, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, iOS may make a diagnostic report. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Those reports contain no personal information.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
