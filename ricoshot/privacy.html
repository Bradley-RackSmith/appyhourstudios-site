Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Ricoshot: Slime Siege is a game for iPhone made by Appy Hour Studios, LLC. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any personal data ourselves. There's no account and no analytics. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show them, as described below.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, gems, Workshop upgrades, Supers, Pip's World, Pip's looks, hats and trails, best waves, stats, daily gift streak and settings are stored on your phone. We never see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads

The game shows an ad between some runs (never in the middle of one) and offers optional videos for rewards. These ads come from Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your IP address, and from it an approximate location;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.

Your choices. For players aged 16 and over, where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the game asks for consent with Google's own consent form the first time it opens. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy choices in the game. The game also shows these players Apple's Allow app to track prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google doesn't receive your advertising identifier and the ads you see are less personalised. You can change this later in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy, including how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between runs. The optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

Purchases

Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and gem packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment details, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, iOS may make a diagnostic report. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Those reports contain no personal information.

Children and younger players

Ricoshot is for players of all ages. The first time it opens, the game asks what year you were born. Your answer stays on your phone and only decides how ads work:

  • Under 13: the game tells Google to treat you as a child. Ads aren't personalised, your device's advertising identifier isn't used, and the game never shows the tracking prompt or the consent form.
  • 13 to 15: ads aren't personalised, and the game doesn't show the tracking prompt or the consent form.
  • Everyone: ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

We collect no personal data from anyone, including children. If you're a parent and have a question, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
