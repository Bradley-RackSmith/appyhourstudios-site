Common questions

Getting started
How does RackSmith pick my exercises?

From the equipment you choose in setup, your goal, the days you train and how long your sessions are. Every exercise and warm-up uses only what's on your equipment list.

Do I need a strength plan?

No. You can skip the plan in setup and use conditioning workouts or workouts you build yourself. When you're ready, tap Build my plan on the Plan tab or in Settings.

I got new equipment, or changed gyms. What do I do?

Go to Settings → Equipment and update your list. The app offers to update your plan in place, so you keep your current week and your workout history.

Can more than one person use the app?

Yes. Each person has their own profile, plan, equipment and history. Tap your name on the Plan tab to switch, or choose Add a new user on the start screen.

Can I use kilograms?

Yes. Go to Settings and choose Kilograms (kg). Weights and plate math switch over.

Workouts and progress
The starting weights look too light or too heavy.

That's expected at first. The first couple of times you do a weighted lift, the app asks after each set how many more reps you could have done, and sets the next set's weight from your answer. You can also change the weight on any set when you log it, and later weeks follow.

When does the weight go up?

When you've earned it: hit your reps on every set and the next session is heavier. For some lifts, and for experienced lifters, you climb the rep range first, and the app shows the rep goal ("aim for 12"). Miss twice in a row and it eases off by about 10%.

How do I swap an exercise?

On the Plan tab, tap Swap under any exercise in a day you haven't done yet. Pick an alternative and choose This session or From now on. Choose the original again to undo it.

How do I see how to do an exercise?

Tap the exercise's name, in your plan or during a workout, to open its guide: the key cue, step-by-step instructions, common mistakes and when to swap it.

How do I build my own workout?

On the Plan tab, under Your workouts, tap Build your own workout. Add exercises, set the sets, reps and rest, and pair exercises into supersets if you like. One saved workout is free; RackSmith Pro saves as many as you like and can make a weekly plan from them.

What is the weekly check-in?

A short set of questions on Sunday evening about how training felt that week. If you've been worn down for two weeks running, it offers a lighter week. Switch it on or off in Settings. It needs a strength plan.

Can I export my workouts?

Yes. Go to History → Insights and tap Export workout history. You get a spreadsheet (CSV) of every set and every conditioning workout, to save to Files, AirDrop or email.

Timers and sound
The rest timer when my phone is locked

The rest timer keeps time while your phone is locked, and a notification tells you when rest is over. If you don't get it, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → RackSmith and make sure notifications are allowed.

With RackSmith Pro, the countdown and your next set also show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. If they don't appear, check that Live Activities is on in iPhone Settings → RackSmith.

Will the beeps stop my music?

No. Countdown beeps play over your music without pausing it. You can turn sound and haptics off in Settings.

Conditioning
What conditioning workouts are there?

EMOM (every minute on the minute), AMRAP (as many rounds as possible), intervals and For Time, built from your equipment. Starter 12, a 12-minute workout with no equipment, is free for everyone. With RackSmith Pro, you can build workouts by length, intensity and format, save them, and add a short finisher after strength days.

How do the workouts get harder?

From your results. Before your first workout, two quick questions set a starting level, and each workout you finish moves the level from there.

Apple Watch and Apple Health
How do I use RackSmith on my Apple Watch?

The Watch app is part of RackSmith Pro. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone (under Available Apps), then start a workout on your iPhone: it appears on your watch with the timer, the set and the buttons. If it doesn't open by itself, open RackSmith on the watch.

How do I save workouts to Apple Health?

Turn on Apple Health in Settings and allow RackSmith to save workouts. Finished strength and conditioning workouts are saved from then on. RackSmith only saves workouts; it doesn't read anything from Health. Apple Health is free.

RackSmith Pro
What does RackSmith Pro add?

More ways to train and more tools around your workouts: the lock screen timer and iCloud backup, Insights and Progress widgets, the conditioning builder and finishers, unlimited workouts of your own and the Apple Watch app. Everything else stays free, and nothing that was free will move to Pro.

Pro is $4.99 a month, or $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial (prices may differ by country). One subscription covers everyone who uses RackSmith on the same iPhone.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose RackSmith Pro. You can also get there from Settings → RackSmith Pro → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid for Pro, but the app doesn't show it.

Open Settings → RackSmith Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with. If that doesn't work, email us. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page, since Apple handles all payments.

Your data
Is my data backed up?

Your workouts are stored on your iPhone. With RackSmith Pro, they're also backed up to your own iCloud after every workout. You can see when in Settings → iCloud backup, or tap Back up now.

On a new phone, or after reinstalling, the start screen offers Restore from iCloud. You can also restore from Settings. Restoring is free, with or without Pro. If backup says iCloud isn't available, sign in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings app and make sure iCloud Drive is on.

Your workouts are stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it. RackSmith doesn't use an account or a server.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, Delete [your name] removes one person and their history. Reset all data removes everyone on the phone. Deleting the app also removes all its data from the phone.

To delete an iCloud backup too, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, manage your storage, choose RackSmith Fitness and delete its data.
