About the app

Most training apps hand you a generic plan and leave you to work out what fits your gym. RackSmith starts from the equipment you have, whether that's a full commercial gym or a pair of dumbbells, and every exercise, warm-up and weight in your plan uses only that equipment.

It doesn't guess your weights either. The first time you do a lift, RackSmith starts light, asks how many more reps you had left and sets the next set from your answer. After that, weight goes up when you hit your reps, and eases off if you miss twice in a row. There's no account, no ads, and your workouts stay on your iPhone.

Features

  • Strength plans built from your equipment: 2 to 6 days a week, 30 to 90 minutes a session.
  • Every set guided: target weight and reps, plate math, warm-up sets and rest timers that beep over your music.
  • Calibration that finds your real working weights, and progression that moves you up when you've earned it.
  • An animated movement guide for every exercise in the library, more than 120 of them.
  • History, personal bests and a strength chart for every lift, a weekly check-in, and CSV export.
  • Build your own workouts, with supersets.
  • Apple Health: finished workouts saved to Health.
  • RackSmith Pro: EMOM, AMRAP, interval and For Time conditioning built from your gear; the Apple Watch app, with heart rate and calories saved to Health; a rest timer on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island; Insights and widgets; and iCloud backup.
