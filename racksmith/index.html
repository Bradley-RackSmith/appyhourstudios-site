Your plan

Built for your gear

Choose the equipment you have, then pick a goal and how often you can train. Every exercise, warm-up and weight in your plan uses only that equipment.

  • Plans from 4 weeks to open-ended, 2 to 6 days a week, 30 to 90 minutes a session.
  • Swap any exercise for one your gear supports, for one session or from then on.
  • Warm-ups that fit the lift, with ramp-up sets worked out for you.
  • Everyone on one iPhone gets their own plan, equipment and history.
Every set, guided

Real weights, earned

No generic guesses. The first time you do a lift, RackSmith asks how many more reps you had left and sets the next set from your answer.

  • Plate math on every set, built on the bars and plates you own.
  • Weights go up when you hit your reps, and ease off if you miss twice in a row.
  • A rest timer with countdown beeps that play over your music.
  • What you lifted last time, right there on the screen.
Conditioning

A second way to train

EMOM, AMRAP, intervals and For Time, built from your equipment in seconds and timed for you, with beeps, cues and a fanfare when the work is done.

  • Pick the length, intensity and format, or let it choose.
  • Workouts step up as you get fitter, based on your results.
  • Optional finishers: a short conditioning block after a strength day.
  • Starter 12 is free for everyone. Building your own workouts is part of Pro.
Movement guides Free

Know every movement

Every exercise in the library, more than 120 of them, has a guide: an animated figure, the key cue, step-by-step instructions, common mistakes and when to swap it.

  • Tap any exercise name in your plan or a workout to open its guide.
  • Motion that follows good coaching: bar paths, tempo and the key positions.
Progress

Watch your strength climb

Personal bests at the end of every session, a strength chart for every exercise and your full history, all on your phone.

  • Insights Pro show hard sets per muscle each week, your streak and how often you train.
  • Export everything as a spreadsheet (CSV), free.
  • Body weight tracking, in pounds or kilograms.
Build your own

Your program, your way

Already follow your own routine? Build it in RackSmith and keep the same guided sets, timers, weights that follow what you lift, and history.

  • Pick exercises, sets, reps and rest, and pair them into supersets.
  • One saved workout is free. Pro saves as many as you like and turns them into a weekly plan.
