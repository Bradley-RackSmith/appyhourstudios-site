Privacy policy

Your data stays yours

Effective November 3, 2026 · applies to version 1.4 and later

RackSmith Fitness doesn't collect your workout data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn on backup.

What the app stores

To build and run your training plan, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Your profile: name, age, sex, height, body weight, experience, goals and training schedule.
  • The equipment you list.
  • Your plans, workout logs and body weight history.
  • Your settings, such as units, sound and haptics.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Save workouts to Apple Health in the app's Settings and allow it, RackSmith Fitness saves each finished workout to Health: the type of workout and when it started and ended.

With the RackSmith Apple Watch app (RackSmith Pro), your watch measures your heart rate and active calories during a workout, and saves them to Health with that workout. To do this, the app asks to read your heart rate and active energy. It reads them only while a workout is running, only to save them with the workout, and doesn't keep them anywhere else.

Health information stays in Apple Health on your devices. RackSmith Fitness doesn't send it to us or to anyone else, doesn't include it in its iCloud backup, and never uses it for advertising or marketing. You can change what RackSmith Fitness may save or read at any time: on your iPhone, open Settings, then Apps, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, then RackSmith Fitness.

Apple Watch (RackSmith Pro)

The RackSmith Apple Watch app shows and controls the workout running on your iPhone. The two talk to each other directly, through Apple's connection between your iPhone and your watch. Nothing goes through a server.

iCloud backup (RackSmith Pro)

With RackSmith Pro, the app can back up the information above to your own iCloud account, in a private area that only RackSmith Fitness on your devices can use. Backups happen after each workout, when you leave the app (at most once an hour), and when you tap Back up now. The backup is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't have access to it.

Restoring a backup is free, with or without Pro, and replaces what's on the phone with the backup.

Purchases (RackSmith Pro)

RackSmith Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple ID.

To confirm who has Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. When the app opens, and when you subscribe or restore a purchase, it sends RevenueCat:

  • A random ID that the app creates. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Your RackSmith purchase and subscription history from Apple.
  • Basic technical details needed to process it, such as the app and iOS version, your App Store country, currency and language.

As with any internet connection, RevenueCat also receives your device's IP address when the app connects. RevenueCat doesn't collect your location.

RevenueCat uses this to confirm subscriptions and to show us anonymous totals, such as how many people subscribe. It never receives your workout data, profile or equipment. It isn't used for advertising or to track you across apps or websites. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

App updates

Besides App Store updates, the app can download small fixes directly from Expo, the service used to build it. When the app opens, it checks for one, sending a random ID for this install (not linked to you), the app's version and the update channel. Expo also receives your device's IP address with the request. No workout data, profile or equipment is sent. See Expo's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't share your workout data with anyone.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't require an account. The app doesn't connect to any server of ours. The only outside services it uses are Apple (the App Store, iCloud and Health), RevenueCat and Expo, as described above.

Notifications and the lock screen

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a notification on your iPhone to tell you when a rest timer ends. With RackSmith Pro, a rest timer also appears on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island during a workout, showing the exercise and your next set. Both are created on your iPhone and don't involve any server. Anyone who can see your lock screen can see what's on it. You can turn Live Activities off for the app in the iPhone Settings app.

Deleting your data

You're in control. In the app's Settings, you can delete one person and their history, or reset all data. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

To delete an iCloud backup, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage, choose RackSmith Fitness and delete its data. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's device backup may also include the app's data, under Apple's privacy policy and your backup settings.

Purchase records held by RevenueCat are tied only to the random ID described above, so we can't connect them to you. If you have a question about them, email us.

Children

RackSmith Fitness isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Health guidance

RackSmith Fitness gives general fitness guidance and isn't medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new training program.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the app version it covers is released.
