Privacy policy

Your data stays yours

Effective October 7, 2026

RackSmith Fitness doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

To build and run your training plan, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • Your profile: name, age, sex, height, body weight, experience, goals and training schedule.
  • The equipment you list.
  • Your plans, workout logs and body weight history.
  • Your settings, such as units, sound and haptics.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking code.
  • We don't require an account, and the app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a notification on your iPhone to tell you when a rest timer ends. These notifications are created on your device and don't involve any server.

Deleting your data

You're in control. In the app's Settings, you can delete one person and their history, or reset all data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's data, under Apple's privacy policy and your backup settings.

Children

RackSmith Fitness isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health guidance

RackSmith Fitness gives general fitness guidance and isn't medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new training program.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
