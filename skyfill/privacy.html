Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Skyfill Squares is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Appy Hour Studios, LLC. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any personal data ourselves. There's no account and no analytics. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show them, as described below.

What stays on your phone

Your levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, styles, settings, the constellations you've completed and the ones you've built yourself are stored on your phone. We never see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads

The game shows an ad between some games and offers optional videos for rewards. These ads come from Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your IP address, and from it an approximate location;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the game asks for your consent with Google's own consent form the first time it opens. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy choices in the game. The game also shows Apple's Allow app to track prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google doesn't receive your advertising identifier and the ads you see are less personalised. You can change this later in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy, including how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack stops the ads between games. The optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

Purchases

Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment details, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, iOS may make a diagnostic report. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Those reports contain no personal information.

Children

We collect no personal data from anyone, including children. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
