How to play

  • Place pieces: drag any of your three pieces onto the board. You get three new ones when all three are placed.
  • Clear lines: fill a whole row or column and it clears. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.
  • Light the constellation: clear a line through a star's square to light that star. Light every star to complete the constellation.
  • Star chains: light a star joined by a line to the last star you lit, and the chain grows for bonus points.
  • Spins: tap a piece in your tray to turn it. You get three free spins each game, plus one for each constellation you complete.
  • Meteor showers: glowing rings show where rocks will land after your next move. Clear the rocks for a bonus.
  • Comet: clearing lines fills the Comet meter. Drop the Comet on any square to clear its row and column.

To see the basics again, open Settings in the game and tap Play the Walkthrough. The full rules are under Settings > How to Play.

Purchases

  • Restoring Remove Ads on a new phone or after reinstalling: in the game, open Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Coins, boosters, styles and the stars in your own constellations are bought with coins and saved on your phone, so they can't be restored from the App Store.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

  • Your levels, stars, coins, boosters, styles and constellations are saved on your phone (and in your iPhone's own backup). They don't sync between devices, and deleting the game resets them.
  • Stuck on a level? Try again: the pieces you're dealt change each time, and boosters and spins can get you out of a tight spot.
