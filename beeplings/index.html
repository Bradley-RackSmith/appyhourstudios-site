Build robot friends.
Learn without words.

A learning game for ages 2 to 6. Kids build, paint, charge and program little robots, then wake them up and watch them roll into town. Every instruction is shown, never spoken or written.

Four stations, four skills

Every robot goes down the workshop line. Each station grows with your child, from first taps to real puzzles.

Build · Shapes

Fit the parts

Drag circles, squares, stars and hexagons onto the robot's outline. Later: look-alike shapes and pieces that are the right shape but the wrong size.

Paint · Colors

Match the picture

A picture card shows the finished robot, and the part to paint glows in the color it needs. Later: close shades like blue and light blue.

Charge · Counting

Power it up

Tap batteries into the meter one by one, then pick the pack that matches a number, all the way to 10.

Code · First coding

Plan the path

Lay arrow cards to walk the robot to its charging pad, press Go, and fix the route if it bumps into something.

Then press the big power button, pick a sticker, and the new friend rolls into town to wander, beep and dance with the others.

Made for parents, too

Everything for grown-ups sits behind a quick multiplication question, out of small hands' reach.

Adapts

Right level, every time

Pick your child's age to start. Each skill steps up when it's easy and eases off when it's hard. There's no way to fail.

Calm

Recharge Time

Set a play limit of 10 to 30 minutes. When it's up, the robots plug in and fall asleep to a lullaby.

Private

Nothing leaves the device

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Progress stays on your iPhone or iPad.

Questions

What ages is it for?

Ages 2 to 6. Two-year-olds tap pieces into place; five- and six-year-olds plan multi-step paths around obstacles. The game adjusts each skill on its own.

Does my child need to read?

No. There are no words to read and no voice instructions. Outlines, glows, picture cards and a pointing hand show what to do. The robots talk in beeps.

What does it cost?

Toy Town is free to play forever. Moon Base, Coral Reef and Dino Park are one-time purchases, or get all three together for less. No subscription and no in-game currency.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays without an internet connection. You only need one to buy or restore a world.
