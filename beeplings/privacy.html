Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Beeplings: Robot Learning is a learning game for young children on iPhone and iPad, made by Appy Hour Studios, LLC. Because it's made for children, we've kept it simple: Beeplings collects no personal information from anyone. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no chat and no links out to the web from the children's part of the app.

What stays on the device

The robots your child has built, their stickers, the level of each skill, the age you chose, the play-time limit and the sound settings are stored on the device only. We never see them. They're included in the device's own backup, and deleting the app deletes them. Start over in the Grown-ups area clears them at any time.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Beeplings contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code. It doesn't use the advertising identifier and never shows the "Allow app to track" prompt. The app doesn't connect to our servers or anyone else's, apart from the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

Moon Base, Coral Reef, Dino Park and All Worlds are bought through the App Store, only from the Grown-ups area behind a parental question. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment or personal details, only Apple's confirmation of which worlds were bought.

The Grown-ups area

Settings, purchases and the support and privacy links sit behind a question that a grown-up types the answer to. This keeps young children from making purchases, changing settings or leaving the app.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, iOS may make a diagnostic report. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your device's Analytics settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Those reports contain no personal information.

Children's privacy

Beeplings is designed for children aged 2 to 6 and is listed in the App Store's Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children (or anyone else), consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
