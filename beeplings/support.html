Support

Something not working, a puzzle that seems too hard or too easy, or an idea for a new world? Get in touch:

Email: beeplings.support@appyhourstudios.com

Please include your iPhone or iPad model and what happened (which station, and roughly what your child was doing).

The Grown-ups area

  • Opening it: on the town screen, tap the small padlock at the top left, then type the answer to the multiplication question. The question keeps young children out of purchases and settings.
  • Age: sets where each station starts. You can change it any time. The game also adjusts each skill on its own as your child plays.
  • Sound: music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch.
  • Progress: shows the level and rounds played for shapes, colors, counting and coding.

Recharge Time (play limit)

  • Choose 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes in Grown-ups, or No limit.
  • When time is up, your child finishes the robot they're building, then the robots plug in and fall asleep to a lullaby.
  • The screen stays on Recharge Time until a grown-up answers the question to keep playing.

Worlds and purchases

  • Toy Town, its 12 robots, and the Robot Jobs and Robot Parade games are free.
  • Each paid world adds 12 robots and its own game in the toy box: Countdown Launch (Moon Base), Bubble Patterns (Coral Reef) and Fossil Dig (Dino Park).
  • Moon Base, Coral Reef and Dino Park are one-time purchases, or buy All Worlds to get all three together. There's no subscription.
  • Family Sharing: worlds are shared with everyone in your family group.
  • Restoring on a new device or after reinstalling: open Grown-ups and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account that bought the world.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • You can also turn off in-app purchases entirely in your device's Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Your child's progress

  • Robots built, stickers and levels are saved on the device (and in its own backup). They don't sync between devices, and deleting the app resets them. Purchased worlds can always be restored.
  • Start over in Grown-ups clears all robots and levels on this device, for a new child or a fresh start. Purchased worlds stay unlocked.

Privacy

Beeplings has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and nothing your child does leaves the device. Read the privacy policy.
