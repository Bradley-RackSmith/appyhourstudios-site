Questions

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. It's built to hold 60 frames a second on an iPhone 12 or newer.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for the Famous Circuits, the Daily Track, your saved courses and the map areas you've downloaded. Building a new course or road needs a connection, once.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch Pro and MFi controllers such as Backbone and Razer Kishi all work, in races and in the menus.

Where does the map come from?

OpenStreetMap, the free map of the world made by volunteers. Land heights come from open elevation data, so hills are real.

What does the game know about me?

Nothing. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress is kept on your phone and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

I bought the Full Garage. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Family members get it too, through Family Sharing.
