About the game

Most racing games ship a handful of tracks. ReRoute Racing uses the whole map. It builds tracks from OpenStreetMap, with the road's real elevation, the buildings beside it and the landmarks along it. Players can race twenty-one built-in circuits, or draw a course on any streets they like and race it in seconds.

The game is about one more try: bronze to platinum trophies set by an expert run on every track, your own best run as a ghost car, and a new Daily Track every day. Four cars and their upgrades are earned by racing, never bought.

Features

  • Twenty-one Famous Circuits: eight real racetracks with their grandstands, gravel traps and named corners; three hill climbs above the treeline; ten city circuits past their landmarks.
  • Draw a Course and Random Circuit: tracks from any streets, from place to place or in laps.
  • Trophies, ghost cars and a Daily Track.
  • A Garage of four cars, earned upgrades, and looks unlocked by level.
  • Synthesized engine sounds, and a car radio that plays your Apple Music playlists.
  • Game controller support, offline play and iCloud saves.
  • No ads, no coins, nothing that buys speed.
