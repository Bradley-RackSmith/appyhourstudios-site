Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Get in touch:

Email: reroute.support@appyhourstudios.com

Please include your iPhone model and the game's version, which is shown at the bottom of the landing screen and in Settings.

Purchases

  • Restoring the Full Garage on a new phone or after reinstalling: in the game, open Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Family Sharing: the Full Garage is shared with your family group automatically.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Maps and building tracks

  • "The map servers are busy right now": the free map services the game uses are under heavy load. Famous Circuits, the Daily Track and your saved courses still work. Try a new road again in a little while.
  • Offline play: Famous Circuits, the Daily Track, saved courses and downloaded map areas all work without a connection. Building a new course or road needs one, once.
  • A road looks wrong: tracks are built from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date. If a road near you is missing or wrong, you can fix it at openstreetmap.org/fixthemap, and the game uses the fix the next time that area is downloaded.

Your progress

  • Your level, cars, best times and courses are saved on your phone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud. They come back automatically on a new phone or after reinstalling.
  • Map areas download again when you need them.

Controls

  • Choose touch controls, and how sensitive the steering is, in Settings > Controls.
  • Game controllers connect in your iPhone's Settings > Bluetooth. The game uses one as soon as it's connected; to drive with touch instead, turn off "Use the controller" in the game's Settings.
